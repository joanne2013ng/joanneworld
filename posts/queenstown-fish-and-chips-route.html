Queenstown Day Plan: Outlets, Fish & Chips and Chur

September 29, 2026

Paul asked where I got my info. I asked which outlets we could visit, and I wanted two places in the same town. My first choice for fish & chips is Chur Fish & Chips on Fernhill Rd. It only opens at 4pm on a Tuesday, though, so here is the plan for today and tomorrow.

Chur Fish & Chips

~17:00 (optional)Chur for an early takeaway dinner. It is quieter just after it opens at 4pm.

TimeWhat
