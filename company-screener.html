Company Screener

A small research tool, ported from Paul's World

Filter a set of well-known companies by sector, size (last reported fiscal-year revenue), and share-price momentum vs. a historical price.

⚠️ Share Price (B), Price Swing (D), and Variance (C) use daily closing prices fetched live from Yahoo Finance's public chart API via a small serverless function on this site — the same approach the original tool uses. "Last Week/Month/Quarter/2–4 Qtr Ago" are approximate trading-day lookbacks (≈5 / 21 / 63 / 126 / 189 / 252 trading days back), not exact calendar dates. Price Swing (D) is the high–low swing from (B)'s point through today. Variance (C) compares today's price (A) directly to (B). This is an unofficial data source and can occasionally be slow, rate-limited, or briefly unavailable — if so, (A), (B), (C), (D) show "—" rather than a guessed value.
